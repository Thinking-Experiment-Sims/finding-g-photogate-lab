Discovery Prompts

1) Steady speed

Load Steady speed. Look at the spacing of the motion map dots.

Question: What does equal spacing tell you about the velocity?

2) Speeding up

Load Speeding up. Predict where the cart will be at t = 4 s before you press Start.

Question: How did the dot spacing change, and why?

3) Moving one way, accelerating the other

Load Turnaround. The cart starts moving right while the acceleration points left.

Question: At the instant the cart turns around, what is its velocity? Its acceleration?

Model Used Here

The cart has constant acceleration, so its motion follows

x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at² and v = v₀ + at

See PHYSICS.md in the repository for derivations and common misconceptions.
