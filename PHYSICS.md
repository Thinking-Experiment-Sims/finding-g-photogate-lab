## 2. Model and assumptions
The cart moves in one dimension with constant acceleration *a*:

$$x(t) = x_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac{1}{2} a t^2 \qquad v(t) = v_0 + a t$$

Assumptions: point object, no friction or air resistance unless stated, *g* = 9.8 m/s². Positive direction: right.

## 5. Worked example
<!-- One fully worked numeric example that matches a test in tests/physics.test.js. -->
A cart starts from rest with *a* = 2 m/s². After 3 s: *x* = ½(2)(3²) = 9 m, *v* = 6 m/s.
